The key points

  • This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics services.
  • Fonts and images are stored on our own web space. No content is loaded from Google or other third parties.
  • The forms do not send anything to us. They turn your entries into an email that you send yourself with your email app.
  • We only pass candidate profiles on to employers with your consent.

2. Visiting the website and hosting

This website is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you visit the website, your browser technically transmits data to the server. This includes in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the address requested and information about your browser and operating system. According to GitHub, the IP address of visitors to a GitHub Pages website is logged and stored for security purposes. We have no access to these logs and do not analyse them.

Purpose and legal basis: The processing serves to deliver the website and to keep it stable and secure. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in providing our website securely and reliably.

Transfer to the USA: GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU US Data Privacy Framework. For transfers to certified companies there is an adequacy decision of the European Commission of 10 July 2023 (Art. 45 GDPR). According to GitHub, it additionally relies on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission.

Retention: GitHub determines how long the logs are kept. For more information, see the GitHub privacy statement.

The connection to this website is encrypted using TLS (https).

3. No cookies, no tracking

We do not set cookies and do not use analytics, tracking or advertising services. We do not embed social media plugins, maps or videos. Fonts and images are loaded from our own web space, not from external providers.

4. Storage in your browser

So that two features of the site work as intended, the website stores small entries in your browser's storage:

  • rm-lang (local storage): remembers the language you chose on the page for technicians. The entry remains until you delete it in your browser.
  • rm-booker-closed (session storage): remembers that you closed the appointment window. The entry is deleted when you close the tab.

These entries are not transmitted to us or to third parties. They are strictly necessary to provide the feature you requested (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete them at any time in your browser settings.

5. Contact by email and phone, requests via the forms

How our forms work: The forms on this website (employer enquiry, appointment request, guide, talent pool and salary check) do not store or send anything themselves. Your browser turns your entries into an email text. You send it yourself with your email app or copy it. We only start processing once you send us the email.

Which data: the information you send us, for example your name, email address, company, phone number and the content of your message, such as the role you are looking for, licence, region or a preferred appointment. If you call us, we process your phone number and the content of the call.

Purpose and legal basis:

  • We handle enquiries from companies about vacancies and appointment requests in order to prepare or perform a contract (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).
  • If you request the guide, we send you the PDF by email (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). We do not use your email address for a newsletter and do not pass it on.
  • We answer other enquiries on the basis of our legitimate interest in handling enquiries (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Retention: We delete the data once your request has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply (see section 8).

6. Candidates: talent pool, salary check and placement

Talent pool: If you join our talent pool, we process the information you send us, such as your name, email address, licence, aircraft types, region, availability and wishes for your next role. We use it to offer you suitable roles and to contact you about them. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). As soon as we work with you on a specific placement, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR is an additional legal basis.

Disclosure to employers: We only pass your details on to an employer, even as a short profile without your name, with your consent. Your current employer will not hear anything from us.

Salary check: We process your salary check details, such as licence, experience, region and salary range, on the basis of your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). We only analyse them in aggregate. Published results contain no information that could identify individuals. We send you the report by email.

Referral bonus: If you recommend a colleague, they contact us themselves. If we pay a bonus, we process your name, your contact details and the information needed for the payment (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR) and keep the records as required by law (Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR).

Withdrawal: You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future; a short email to kontakt@ressel-mueller.de is enough (Art. 7 (3) GDPR). This does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Retention: We store your talent pool profile until you withdraw your consent. If we have had no contact for 24 months, we delete your profile unless you have agreed to further storage. We delete individual salary check entries once the report has been prepared, at the latest after 24 months. If a placement takes place, we keep the documents needed for invoicing as required by law.

7. Recipients and service providers

  • Email hosting: Our mailboxes are operated on our behalf by Zoho Corporation B.V., Utrecht, Netherlands (Art. 28 GDPR). The data is stored in a data centre in the EU. According to Zoho, staff in India may access it for technical support. Standard contractual clauses of the European Commission are in place for this (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR).
  • Website hosting: GitHub, Inc., see section 2.
  • Employers: only with your consent, see section 6.
  • Tax advisers and authorities: Our tax adviser receives data on invoices and payments where this is necessary for accounting and tax purposes. Authorities only receive data where we are legally obliged to provide it.

8. Retention periods and statutory obligations

We only store personal data for as long as necessary for the respective purpose or as long as you have consented. We are required by law to keep business letters, invoices and accounting records, in particular under § 147 of the German Fiscal Code, for up to ten years depending on the document. During this period, we restrict processing to storage.

9. Your rights

You have the right to

  • access the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • withdraw any consent given with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR).

Right to object under Art. 21 GDPR

Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. We will then no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

For all requests, an email to kontakt@ressel-mueller.de is enough.

Right to lodge a complaint: You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is:

10. Provision of data and automated decisions

You are under no legal or contractual obligation to provide us with data. Without your details, however, we cannot answer your request or place you in a role. There is no automated decision making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.
